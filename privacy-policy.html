Privacy Policy for RadioKu

Last updated: September 24, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how RadioKu ("the App") handles information when you use the application.

RadioKu is an internet radio application that allows users to discover, search, and listen to radio stations through internet streaming services.

1. Information We Collect

RadioKu does not require users to create an account and does not directly ask users to provide personal information such as their name, address, telephone number, or email address in order to use the main features of the application.

However, third-party services used by the App, including Google AdMob and radio streaming services, may process certain information automatically as described below.

2. Advertising

RadioKu uses Google AdMob to display advertisements, including banner advertisements and native advertisements.

Google AdMob may automatically collect or process information such as device information, advertising identifiers, IP address, and information related to advertising and app usage, depending on applicable settings, user choices, and regional requirements.

This information may be used to provide, measure, and improve advertising services, including personalized or non-personalized advertising where applicable.

For more information about how Google uses information from apps that use its services, please visit:

How Google uses information from sites or apps that use our services

3. Radio Streaming Services

RadioKu retrieves information about radio stations from third-party internet radio services and connects to radio streaming servers selected by the user.

When you select and play a radio station, your device connects to the relevant streaming server. The streaming provider may process technical information such as your IP address and connection information according to its own privacy policy.

RadioKu does not control the privacy practices of third-party radio streaming providers.

4. Search and Radio Station Data

RadioKu provides a search function that allows users to search for available radio stations by name.

The search text entered by the user is used to filter the radio station list within the application. RadioKu does not require users to submit their personal identity in order to use this feature.

5. Internet Access

RadioKu requires internet access to retrieve radio station information, connect to radio streaming services, and display advertisements.

Without an internet connection, some or all of the App's features may not function properly.

6. Permissions

RadioKu does not require access to your contacts, camera, microphone, photos, or precise location for its main radio streaming functionality.

7. Data Sharing

RadioKu does not sell users' personal information.

Information may be processed by third-party service providers required for the operation of the App, including advertising services and internet radio streaming services.

Such third-party services may process technical information in accordance with their own privacy policies and applicable laws.

8. Children's Privacy

RadioKu is not specifically directed toward children under the age of 13.

We do not knowingly collect personal information directly from children through the App.

9. Data Security

We take reasonable measures to operate the App securely. However, no method of transmission over the internet or method of electronic storage can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

10. Third-Party Services

RadioKu may use third-party services that have their own privacy policies. These services may include:

  • Google AdMob
  • Internet radio streaming providers
  • Radio station directory or metadata services

Users are encouraged to review the privacy policies of these third-party services for additional information about their data practices.

11. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect changes to the App, services used by the App, or applicable legal and regulatory requirements.

Any changes will be published on this page. The "Last updated" date at the top of this Privacy Policy will also be updated when appropriate.

12. Contact

If you have questions, concerns, or requests regarding this Privacy Policy or the privacy practices of RadioKu, please contact the developer through the contact information provided on the official RadioKu or developer page.
